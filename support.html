Common questions

How do I connect a second device (like my child's tablet) to the same household?
On the first device, open Settings and go to Household to find your household code. On the second device, choose "Join an Existing Household" when the app first opens, and enter that exact code.

Do I need to create an account?
No — there are no usernames, passwords, or emails required to use the app itself. Devices connect to each other using a private household code you generate.

How do rewards work?
You can set up rewards in Settings that unlock based on completing specific tasks, finishing all of a day's tasks, or keeping a streak of fully completed days going.

Is my family's data private?
Yes. See our Privacy Policy for full details — in short, we don't collect names, emails, or any identifying information, and we never show ads or sell data.
